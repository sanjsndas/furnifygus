What Are Cookies?

Cookies are diminutive text files transferred from a website to your web browser. Their primary function is to enable our site to recall your activities and chosen settings—like login credentials, language selections, and other customizations—for a specified duration. This eliminates the need for you to repeatedly input these details upon subsequent visits or when navigating between different pages. Essentially, cookies are instrumental in elevating your user experience and delivering a more personalized browsing environment.

These files manifest in diverse types, each serving distinct objectives. Certain cookies are indispensable for the fundamental technical functionality of the website, whereas others contribute to enhancing performance, scrutinizing user behavior, and facilitating marketing initiatives.
